Our technology

Iteration Room is a production studio for game makers. You make the creative decisions, and it builds and tests your game with you, one iteration at a time.

Making a game is about more than building its individual parts. It’s about bringing hundreds of interconnected systems together to create a coherent, engaging experience.

Established studios achieve this through experienced multidisciplinary teams, shared creative direction, proven technology, established production practices and continuous iteration and testing. AI is making game development more accessible, but without these foundations, maintaining quality and staying true to the original creative vision remains a significant challenge.

Iteration Room brings the production capabilities of a game studio into the hands of individual developers and small teams. Working alongside you within your existing game engine, it helps you build, integrate, test and refine your game. You remain in control of the creative and technical decisions, with the tools, structure and support to bring your vision to life.

Iteration Room is our working title.

01How it works

Start with strong foundations. Every game begins with a playable foundation, where the world, characters and core gameplay systems are designed to work together. Rather than starting from an empty project, you have something meaningful to build upon.

Establish your creative direction. Define what makes your game unique: how it should feel, how its systems interact and what experience you want to create for your players. These decisions guide development as your game evolves.

Play, collaborate and refine. Work with Iteration Room to explore ideas, experiment with mechanics and refine the experience. Changes are evaluated against your design intent and supported by continuous testing, helping you maintain coherence and quality throughout development.

We want developers to have more room to experiment, follow their curiosity and take creative risks, with the foundations in place to support them as their game evolves.

02Who it’s for

We are building Iteration Room first for experienced game developers and small studios who want to achieve more without compromising their craft or creative independence.

Our aim is to give these teams the freedom to explore more ambitious ideas, experiment with confidence and spend more time on the creative decisions that make their games unique.

Over time, we want to make these capabilities accessible to a wider community of creators, expanding across genres and lowering the technical barriers to making games.

03Made with Iteration Room

We are game developers ourselves, and Iteration Room is an integral part of how we make our own games.

Once Upon a Well, a fairy-tale adventure where your answers shape how each story unfolds, grew from the living world we created for Village of Whispers.

Building our own games allows us to continuously challenge, validate and evolve the technology through real production experience.
