Making games, and the technology to make them

Photographed by Bulut Karakaya

We make games, and we build the technology that helps more people make theirs.

Second River Games is a team of experienced game developers. Between us we have shipped games from mobile to triple-A, led teams in studios large and small, and trained developers across the industry in making games with AI.

We are passionate about turning that experience into something anyone with a good idea can use: the craft, the process and the know-how that usually only a studio has.

So we work on both sides: our own games, and the technology to make them. Each makes the other better.

Founded in Cambridge, UK.
