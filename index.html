Second River Games

UK-based game studio making simulation and management games

Photographed by Bulut Karakaya

We combine traditional game development with cutting-edge technologies to create worlds that surprise players, where characters, systems, and stories interact in unexpected and interesting ways.

At the heart of our games are carefully designed systems, choices with consequences, and handcrafted worlds, and we lean on AI where it meaningfully improves the experience: helping our games better understand player intent, improving communication and giving our small team more time to focus on creativity, design, and play.

Founded in Cambridge, UK by two experienced game developers exploring the edges of simulation, narrative, play and AI.
