# Our games | Second River Games

Our games, made with Iteration Room: Village of Whispers, our living world, and Once Upon a Well, a fairy-tale village where your answers decide how each tale ends.

## Village of Whispers: our living world

A living village whose people remember what you say, and the foundation our games grow from.

The villagers love, argue, remember, and gossip. When you speak, they do not simply obey. They interpret you, spread the word, distort it, and slowly your words become their culture. Belief becomes behaviour, and behaviour decides who leads, who survives, and what the village becomes.

Village of Whispers is where every game we make starts. It is our first playable foundation in Iteration Room: a world, characters and systems that already work together, ready for a new idea.

## Once Upon a Well: our first game from it

You are the wishing well at the heart of a fairy-tale village. The villagers bring you the questions fairy tales never answer. Why does a woman live alone in the woods, in a house made of cake? You answer in one sentence: because she is the best baker in the kingdom. By spring, Hansel and Gretel are running her deliveries.

The stories everyone knows arrive in the village one by one, and your answers decide how each tale ends. Every villager hears your words differently, so the same answer can start a ritual in one household and a feud in the next. The village's storybook rewrites itself as you play, and no two villages end the same way.

We built it from Village of Whispers with Iteration Room. Chapter one exists as a playable demo, which we show at events and to partners.

That is all we will say for now. More will come later, soon.
